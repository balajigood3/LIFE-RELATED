# GIS Safety & Isolation Notes (Demo)

## SF6 Pressure Alarms
- Stage 1 Low: Investigate leak, prepare for isolation.
- Stage 2 Low: Block closing, initiate LOTO, do not operate breaker.

## Isolation Sequence (Typical)
1. Trip / open circuit breaker
2. Open disconnectors both sides
3. Close earthing switches
4. Verify zero energy
5. Apply personal locks

## IEC 60079 Reference
Work in Zone 1 requires Ex-rated tools and competent persons. Live working generally prohibited.
